# Synthetic Type 2 Diabetes Management Guideline

## 1. Scope

This synthetic guideline addresses adults with newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes
and an HbA1c measurement available at the initial visit.

## 2.1 Glycemic Treatment Decision

When HbA1c is 9 or higher, initiate combination pharmacotherapy. When HbA1c is
below 9, begin metformin together with lifestyle support.

### Decision Table 1: Glycemic Treatment Decision

| HbA1c | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| >= 9 | Combination therapy |
| < 9 | Metformin plus lifestyle |

## 2.2 Diabetes Monitoring Plan

Patients receiving combination therapy should repeat HbA1c monitoring in 3
months. Patients receiving metformin plus lifestyle support should repeat HbA1c
monitoring in 6 months.

### Decision Table 2: Diabetes Monitoring Plan

| Treatment | Follow-up months |
| --- | --- |
| Combination therapy | 3 |
| Metformin plus lifestyle | 6 |
